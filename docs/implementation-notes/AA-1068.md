# AA-1068 — CRM finance: trip P&L, Bookings with AA, supplier bills, profit reports, group departure P&L

Lane l5 of S276. Source: the S275 study of a tour and travel app (root
`docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`, items crm-25, crm-26, crm-30, crm-31, crm-32). Only
the app's behaviour was read; no code was copied (OPL-1). Built with the proposed defaults; the business owner's
answers may change defaults later.

## Decisions

- **One contract migration, crm-051** (the cancellation and payment schedule lane of the wave takes crm-049 and
  crm-050 and merges first; the view reads its `crm.cancellation_charge`): ALTER of `crm.trip`
  (`group_code`) and `crm.booking_line_money` (kind `actual_cost`, FX basis `bill_date`), four base-template tables
  (`supplier_bill`, `supplier_bill_line`, `aa_invoice_cache`, `departure_cost`), two views (`v_trip_pnl`,
  `v_departure_trip`), the definer `crm.booking_line_actual_cost`, the guard `crm.guard_supplier_bill_line` (SQLSTATE
  CRM51) and the `supplier_bill` status moves. MIG-4: migration-only PR, then the code PR.
- **The P&L is one view** (`crm.v_trip_pnl`, security_invoker) so the trip card, the cube and its CSV always agree.
  Revenue = issued invoices net of tax (`subtotal_amount`) by kind; refunds net of tax by the invoice's net share
  (`refund x subtotal / total`); each converted at `crm.fx_resolve` of its issue / receipt day. Every invoice kind
  other than `service_fee` / `cancellation_fee` is a sale (deposit, instalment, balance, adjustment, any later kind).
- **A cancelled trip counts its charge, once** (resumed run): `crm.cancellation_charge` (crm-049) is the seam, fee =
  `charge_amount`. Once a trip has that row, the view counts the charge net of tax (by the net share of the trip's
  issued invoices in the charge currency, 1 when none) at the cancel day's rate **in place of** its sale invoices,
  their refunds and its `cancellation_fee` invoices; service fee invoices still count. Why: the fee invoice bills only
  `charge - kept`, and a kept sale invoice can stay open with an unpaid rest, so invoices minus refunds over-count a
  cancelled trip (1000 + tax invoiced, 200 paid, charge 300: invoices say 1100, the charge says 275 net). A waived
  charge is 0. `cancellation_charged` on the row and the card note `cancellation_charge` say so.
- **AA cost**: the quote's frozen cost; once the AA settlement invoice bills the booking **in full** (a `balance` or
  `full` line), the invoiced amount at the quote's FX rate. A deposit-only invoice never replaces the quote (it would
  look like a 70 % saving). Cancellation fee lines of the invoice are left out (Booking reports them as the line's
  `cancellation_cost`, AA-957), so they are never counted twice.
- **Agency cost**: `actual_cost` while a live (not void) bill names the line, else the planned cost. A cancelled agency
  line still billed by its supplier counts its billed cost. Variance = actual - planned of the billed lines only.
- **actual_cost at bill FX**: one chain per line (append-only); the head = the sum of the line's live bill lines in one
  currency (a second currency is refused, CRM51 `bill_currency_mixed`), at the latest bill's frozen rate. A voided bill
  leaves its head in the chain; the view and the line view ignore it while no live bill names the line.
- **Bills never change** after they are recorded (column grants: `status`, `paid_on`, `note`): a wrong bill is voided
  and recorded again. The lines' sum may be under the bill total (the rest is not a trip cost); over it is 422.
- **AA invoice copy** (`crm.aa_invoice_cache`): the issue asks for it; Booking answers `no-store` and its notes say the
  CRM must not cache invoice answers. Resolution: the page reads Booking live on every open and keeps only the summary
  plus, per AA booking, the invoiced amount and whether it is complete (what the P&L and the mismatch flag need, and
  what the page shows when Booking is down). Lines, payments and the issuer text are read live only. Booking's status
  is mirrored as `aa_status` (no CRM state machine, so not a `status` column under the template rule).
- **Erasure by cascade** (no `crm.erase_subject` change): bills, the AA copy and departure costs cascade from their
  `crm.agency_supplier` row (the AA copy from the agency's built-in AA row), a bill line from its bill and its booking
  line. Listed in `CASCADE_ERASED` of `tests/integration/test_crm010_schema.py`.
- **Margin policy on writes too**: the four tables carry a RESTRICTIVE policy FOR ALL (not only SELECT like crm-014),
  so the assistant cannot write a bill or a departure cost even through a bug in a route.
- **Departure refs**: an AA `departure_id` of a live AA line or the trip's own `group_code`. The per-departure split is
  by pax (adults + children) with cumulative rounding in trip id order, so the shares add up to the cost exactly. Lost
  and cancelled trips take no share. A cost converts at the rate of its day (today's rate for a future day).
- **Home "today" tiles**: counts only, every role. Passport warnings check presence (`passport_no_enc`,
  `passport_expiry_enc` IS NULL) on trips leaving within 60 days; the expiry date itself stays encrypted (no
  decryption for a count). Visa checks wait for their own card.
- **Profit cube window**: trips that travel in the report window (start date, else the planned month), booked or later
  (cancelled ones too: they may keep fees and costs). `product` splits each trip's confirmed lines into AA tours vs the
  agency's own services (line sell - line cost), because revenue is invoiced per trip, not per line.

## Changed

- Not built here, by design of the issue: the departure roster page (crm-22, a later card) — the API
  `GET /departures/{ref}/pnl` and a simple `/departures/[ref]` page are the seam; the sales partner commission (crm-28)
  — `v_trip_pnl.partner_commission_base` is 0 and `sales_partner_id` NULL, the cube's `sales_partner` dimension shows
  "No partner" until then; AA's guaranteed status of a departure — the Booking sync ignores `departure.guaranteed`
  today, so the tile's `guaranteed` is null.
- `app/ledger/lines.py`: the line money view drops an `actual_cost` head of a line no live bill names and adds
  `cost_variance_base` / `actual_margin_base` while billed (one extra query per trip lines read, only for owner /
  agent).
- `app/reports/export.py` + `app/routers/reports.py`: the `profit` CSV section with `dimension=`.
- `app/ledger/suppliers.py`: a supplier named by a departure cost is not moved to the trash (409 `supplier_in_use`).

## Tradeoffs

- Revenue FX per invoice through `crm.fx_resolve` in the view (a plpgsql call per invoice and refund): fine for an
  agency's few hundred trips; a materialised rate per invoice would need a change to invoices (not this card).
- The AA copy is refreshed only when someone opens the page (and the invoice detail); a background refresh job was not
  added (no new job kind for a page the owner opens weekly). The Home "overdue AA invoices" tile reads the copy.
- The trip page reads the P&L in a second round after the page's batch (keeps the change to the shared trip page small).
