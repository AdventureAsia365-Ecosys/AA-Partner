# AA-1071 — sales partner commission payable and the partner's own pages

S275 study items crm-28 (sub-agent / referral commission payable) and crm-29 (sub-agent portal); root
`docs/research/2026-11-10-odoo-tour-travel-plugin-comparison.md`. Behaviour only, no plugin code. Migration crm-056
(contract, MIG-4), then the code PR. Built with the proposed defaults; the business owner's answer to the study's
question 5 (percent, fixed per traveller or net rate) only changes defaults.

## Decisions

- **Own link table, not `crm.client_link`.** The issue proposed a `client_link` kind `partner_intake`. `client_link`
  needs a trip and a traveller (NOT NULL, CHECK), expires within 30 days and carries the trip hold guard; a partner's
  link belongs to no trip and lives for months. `crm.sales_partner_link` (purpose `intake` | `view`, token SHA-256,
  at most 400 days, one active per partner and purpose, lookup policy on `app.partner_link_hash`) keeps
  `client_link` and its erase / hold rules untouched.
- **The intake link uses an agency inquiry form.** The owner picks one of the agency's active forms when issuing the
  link; the partner page shows that form and stores the submission the same way (same checks, same order), with
  `form_submission.sales_partner_id`. `crm_intake_process` copies it to the new trip (lead source `sales_partner`),
  or onto an open trip that has no partner yet. A partner deactivated since the inquiry stamps nothing.
- **The partner view signs in with a signed link + an e-mail code** (like `/c/t`). The code mail goes from the
  agency's mailbox (the agency-level one first) through the normal dispatcher: `crm.conversation.sales_partner_id`
  (a partner thread, never Unmatched) and `crm.dispatch_queue.sales_partner_id` (traveler_id now nullable; exactly
  one subject). The stored mail shows the code masked; the code waits on `crm.sales_partner_otp.code_enc` until the
  sender puts it in (the traveller portal's model). Dev echo as the portal (`PORTAL_OTP_DEV_ECHO`).
- **No traveller PII in the view.** The partner sees a short trip reference (`T-` + 8 hex of the trip id), received
  date, start month, traveller count, a stage in four words (enquiry, quoted, booked, travelling, completed,
  closed) and its own commission; never the title (it can name a person), names or contacts.
- **Commission accrues in the database.** `crm.partner_commission_sync(trip)` (SECURITY DEFINER) runs from a trigger
  on `crm.trip` (stage, partner, trash), so every path to `booked` (moves, the deposit, the bulk move, Undo) accrues
  or cancels it; the assistant may book a trip but reads no money. One live row per trip and partner; an accrued row
  is refreshed while it is accrued; approved / paid rows are never touched by the sync.
- **Basis.** `percent` / `net_rate`: rate % of the basis. `revenue` = the accepted proposal's total
  (`accepted_total_amount`, else `total_amount`) in its currency; no accepted proposal (a booking by hand) = the
  issued revenue of `crm.v_trip_pnl` in the base currency. `margin` = `v_trip_pnl.margin_base + partner_commission_base`
  (the margin before partner commissions, never below 0), base currency. `fixed_per_pax` = rate x pax in the
  partner's currency. `amount_base` at `crm.fx_resolve` of the computing day; no rate = left out of the P&L.
- **Net rate** is computed like a percent (the partner keeps rate % of the basis); the difference is only how it is
  settled (by deduction). Kept as its own type so the statement says it; to change after the business owner answers.
- **Approval is the owner's** (`partner_commission_approve`, DB guard CRM54, the owner approves as themselves).
  Approve recalculates first (the margin moves as bills arrive) and refuses if the trip changed in between.
- **Trip cancel** cancels the open (accrued / approved) partner commission unless the member ticks "Keep the
  partner's commission"; a paid one stays. Undo out of booked / trash / partner change cancels only accrued rows.
- **Partner management is owner-only** (`sales_partner_manage`): terms are money. Every member lists partner names
  (the trip picker). Owner and agent see terms, open totals and commissions (`view_margin`).
- **Deletion.** A partner that a trip, a conversation or a dispatch row names is deactivated, never deleted (409
  `partner_in_use`); clearing the contact e-mail and phone removes its personal data beyond the name. Rows go with
  the agency (cascade from `tenant_settings`; links from their form; commissions from their trip), so
  `crm.erase_subject` is unchanged.
- **SQLSTATE CRM54** (crm-054 used CRM53). The CRM-07x lane of the same wave must take CRM55+.

## Changed

- `crm.v_trip_pnl` is replaced with the same columns: `sales_partner_id` = the trip's, `partner_commission_base` =
  its live partner commissions (the crm-051 seam).
- `crm.dispatch_queue_gate_chk` gains `partner_inactive` (the dispatcher drops a mail to an inactive / trashed
  partner).
- The mail sender, the dispatcher gate, the inbound / Unmatched queries and the conversation list know the partner
  subject (`sales_partner_id IS NULL` on every client-conversation query).
- The stage-rules job also expires and purges partner codes after 24 h.

## Tradeoffs

- A dispatcher extension (one more subject kind) instead of a partner-only send path: one way out of the agency's
  mailbox, the same held / failed / send-uncertain states, no second credential decrypt site.
- The partner thread is invisible in the inbox (it holds only masked code mails). A partner's reply threads onto it
  and is not shown; partners are told to contact the agency directly.
- Statement CSV is generated per partner on request (no stored statement); the API sets the formula guard on cells.
