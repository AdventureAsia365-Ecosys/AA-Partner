# AA-1126 — AI spend controls: the AA-Partner side (slices L1 UI, L3, G3, L4)

## Decisions

- **The agency AI budget comes from its CRM plan (L3).** CIS 447 adds the plan feature `crm.ai_budget_usd_monthly` (a
  limit on the CIS Plans page, a per-tenant override on Tenant 360). `crm_entitlement_pull` projects it into
  `crm.tenant_entitlement.ai_budget_usd` (crm-076). When set it is the agency's ceiling and the budget of an agency
  with no settings row; the platform settings `crm.ai.budget_default_usd` / `crm.ai.budget_max_usd` are the fallback
  (an agency not projected yet, a plan without the key, a plan limit of "no limit").
- **The owner's budget follows the plan** while it still equals the previous plan budget (or the platform default
  before the first plan budget): one guarded `UPDATE ... WHERE ai_budget_usd = <previous>`, audited
  `ai_budget_follows_plan`. A budget the owner chose otherwise stays; the new ceiling still caps it.
- **Spend rules per environment (G3 / L4):** setting `crm.spend.environment` (dev | prod, crm-076, CIS Platform >
  Settings, CRM tab, maker-checker, EN + VI guide). prod: the CRM app's daily AI budget (AI gateway > App profiles)
  only alerts (an error log line) and an unreadable month or day spend lets the draft go on (an error log line). The
  agency's own monthly budget stays hard in both.
- **UI (L1 + L3):** the Content studio (in this web app since R3) reads the CIS `job_stop_code` (`ai_quota_exhausted`)
  of a held version: status "AI allowance used up" (not "Writing failed"), an "Upgrade plan" link to the plan page
  (`/portal/account/billing`) and a Retry that stays off; a rewrite or Retry refused with 402 says the real reason; the
  bell words the two allowance notices (`ai.allowance_used`, `tripplanner.ai_allowance_used`) in the UI language and
  links to the plan page. The CRM says "Upgrade your plan" (Settings > Add-ons) instead of "contact AA support" in
  Settings > AI and in the draft panel; `app_budget_exhausted` and `spend_unreadable` get their own sentences.

## Tradeoffs

- The CRM upgrade is an e-mail request, not a self-service plan switch: the CRM has no plan page and plan changes are
  staff actions in CIS (Plans page, Tenant 360). Follow-up when self-service plan changes exist: point
  `planUpgradeHref` at it.

- The seeded plan values equal today's flat 2 USD, so nothing changes on apply; the commercial values are the business
  owner's (CIS Plans page).
- A plan budget that cannot be read (a role without the grant, crm-076 not applied) falls back to the settings inside a
  savepoint rather than failing the call.
- "Bell" for a degraded read in the CRM is an error log line: the CRM has no staff bell; the CIS ops alerting reads the
  logs.
